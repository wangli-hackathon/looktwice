Looktwice is a student project for Arbiter Hacks V1, track Rethinking Waste. The Battery fire check was added during SafeSpace Hacks (November 2026). It is decision support from a November 2026 snapshot of public rules, not an instruction from your hauler. When the cart and the app disagree, believe the cart and the city page. It is not an emergency service: if a battery is smoking or on fire, call 911.

Rethinking waste

Reuse it if it still has a job. Otherwise sort it with San Francisco rules, then keep a log that says where the kilograms and the CO2e came from.

Battery fire check

New · built during SafeSpace Hacks, Nov 2026

Hidden batteries start fires in garbage trucks and sorting plants. Anything that might hold one gets asked first, and “Not sure” counts as yes.
