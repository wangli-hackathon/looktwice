# Demo script — 2 to 3 minutes

Record the screen at 1920×1080 or a phone-width window. Start on the Decide page with the city set to San Francisco and the vision checkbox off. Empty the log first so the numbers belong to this take. Speak the lines, or cut them shorter if you run long. Target about 2 minutes 40 seconds.

## 0:00 — The problem (20 seconds)

“Most waste apps answer with a bin. The better question is whether this should be waste at all, and the bin depends on the city. Looktwice does those in that order, and it will not invent a climate number when EPA’s model has a blank.”

Show the headline. Do not tour the navigation yet.

## 0:20 — Same box, different city (40 seconds)

Click the greasy pizza box.

“Reuse will not do much. The box is already greasy. In San Francisco it is the green compost cart, not the blue bin.”

Point at the climate line.

“WARM has no composting factor for corrugated cardboard. The food-waste number is not borrowed. Mass will count. CO2e stays blank.”

Change the city to “No city picked.”

“Same box. No organics cart assumed, so the baseline is trash. Do not put grease in the recycling and hope.”

Set the city back to San Francisco. Click “Log compost.” Leave the grams. Click “Add to my log.”

## 1:00 — Reuse is the larger number (40 seconds)

Click “Fruit or vegetable” or type leftovers and open Leftovers.

“For food, reuse means it got eaten. Two hundred fifty grams, eaten, against the same plate composted.”

Point at “Same weight, two paths.”

“Eating it is source reduction. Composting avoids landfill methane and is still the smaller credit. Compost is what you do with what nobody can eat.”

Click “Keep it in use.” Log it.

## 1:40 — The battery that is not one rule (30 seconds)

Open Alkaline battery. San Francisco says drop-off.

Switch to New York City.

“New York lets household alkaline cells go in the trash. Tape the big ones. Lithium is a different object, and it is illegal in the trash there.”

Switch to Seattle.

“Seattle bans alkaline cells from the garbage. The recycling cart is the wrong answer in every city. They start fires.”

Do not log unless you have time.

## 2:10 — The log (25 seconds)

Open Log.

“Diverted kilograms include the pizza box even though its CO2e is blank. The climate total is only the paths WARM can support. The blank mass is called out so it cannot hide inside the number.”

If the log is thin, click “Add a demo week” and point at the shirt and the battery, both unestimated, and the can, which is a small credit per can and a large one per kilogram.

## 2:35 — Close (15 seconds)

Open Method for one sentence, or stay on the log.

“The hierarchy is reuse, then the city’s cart, then landfill. The math is allowed to disagree with the cart. This is a November 2026 snapshot of public pages, not your hauler. When they disagree, believe the cart.”

Stop. Total around 2:50.

## If something breaks

- Vision is off on purpose. Do not turn it on unless you have already downloaded the model once.
- If the camera is blocked, use the sample tray. That path does not use a network.
- If a city looks wrong on the day, open the source link under the result and say the snapshot date out loud. Do not pretend it is live.
