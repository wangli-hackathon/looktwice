11:T653,Looktwice measures impact as mass diverted from the landfill and as greenhouse-gas emissions relative to landfilling, using the US Environmental Protection Agency Waste Reduction Model, version 16 (December 2023). For a logged decision, the app takes the WARM net emission factor for the path actually chosen — source reduction at the current mix of inputs when reuse avoids a new purchase, recycling, or composting — subtracts that material’s WARM landfilling factor, and converts the difference from metric tons of CO2e per short ton into kilograms of CO2e with the factor 1 short ton = 907.18474 kg. A negative result is reported as emissions avoided. Kilograms still count as diverted when you reuse, recycle, compost, or use a real drop-off even if WARM has no factor: plastic film, composted cardboard, clothing, ceramics, and household batteries are in that group on purpose, so the climate total cannot be inflated with a borrowed number. Food is entered on a wet-weight basis, which is how WARM states it, and the default grams are editable reference weights rather than measurements. The mile comparison divides avoided kilograms by 0.393 kg CO2e per mile, the EPA equivalency for a gasoline passenger vehicle in the November 2024 calculator update. The arithmetic is there to keep the order of operations honest: eating edible food or keeping a phone in use moves the WARM numbers more than parking the same object in the right cart, and composting mixed yard trimmings does not even win on greenhouse gases against landfilling.
